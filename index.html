FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services does Velrixa Studio offer?

Velrixa Studio offers website design, SEO optimization, AI automation, brand identity, video editing, and social media marketing. Every service is focused on helping businesses look premium online, attract the right audience, and convert visitors into customers.

Does Velrixa Studio work with international clients?

Yes. Velrixa Studio works with clients worldwide, with a strong focus on businesses and individuals in the USA. Communication happens over WhatsApp, email, and video calls, so time zones are never a barrier to getting quality work delivered.

How do I start a project with Velrixa Studio?

Starting is simple: reach out through the contact section or WhatsApp, share your goals, and you will receive a clear plan with scope, timeline, and pricing. Once approved, work begins with regular updates until delivery.

Does Velrixa Studio offer SEO audits?

Yes. Velrixa Studio provides detailed SEO audits that uncover technical errors, content gaps, and authority issues holding your website back. Each audit comes with a prioritized action plan you can implement yourself or have the studio handle.
